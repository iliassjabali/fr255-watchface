FR255 Layout Variations

Layouts for your Forerunner 255 watch face, with the Sky blue accent and the white energy icon that you selected. The first six add the prayer time to the current watch face. The next nine are variations of M1. The next seven are the Minutes layout with three rings. The next ten are variations of Sun and Rings with more data. Give the code of the layout that you want.

The 22 layouts from before, for reference.

Each dial is drawn on the 260 × 260 pixel screen with colors from the 64-color palette of the display. The fonts are close to the watch fonts, not identical.
