Watch face for the Garmin Forerunner 255, with the official Habous prayer times of Morocco.

This release changes the watch face. The Worker that gives the prayer times is the same as in v1.1.0. To get the changes, install the new file on the watch.

## What is new

### Watch face

The bottom of the face now has two lines below the next calendar event.

- **Phone connection.** A dot replaces the phone icon. The dot is a blue disc when the phone is connected, and a gray ring when it is not.
- **High and low temperature.** The weather shows the temperature, then the high and the low of the day, each with an arrow.
- **Next sunrise or sunset.** A second line shows the time of the next sunrise or sunset. The times are the Chourouk and the Maghrib of the same timetable as the prayers. After the Maghrib, the line shows the sunrise of tomorrow.
- **Removed.** The battery days and the alarm bell do not show.

### Documentation

- `design/status-picker.html` is an interactive page to select the content of the two lines. It tells you if a line is too wide.
- The image of the watch face in the README shows the two lines.

## Install

`fr255-watchface.zip` contains the watch face (`fr255-watchface.prg`) and the install steps (`INSTALL.txt`).

1. Connect the watch to the computer with a USB data cable.
2. Copy `fr255-watchface.prg` into the folder `GARMIN/APPS` of the watch. On macOS, use OpenMTP or Android File Transfer, and close Garmin Express first.
3. Disconnect the watch.
4. On the watch, hold the UP button, select "Watch Face", then select "FR255 Test Face".

## Compatibility

- The file is for the standard Forerunner 255. The 255 Music, the 255s and the 255s Music need their own build.
- The official Habous times show for a position 25 km or less from a listed city of Morocco. In all other cases, the watch calculates the times and shows them with the mark `~`.
- The JSON contract between the watch and the Worker did not change.

## Known limits

- A calculated sunrise or sunset has no mark `~`, because the second line has no space for it. The prayer time keeps its mark.
- The high and the low show only when the watch has the two values. With no weather data, the first line shows only the dot.
- The watch face does not show the battery. The battery level is in the controls menu of the watch.
- The prayer names show only in Arabic.
- The watch face does not show the seconds.
- A correction that Habous publishes in a month can stay invisible until the end of that month, because the Worker keeps the month file.
- The CI cannot run the Monkey C unit tests. It only proves that they compile.
